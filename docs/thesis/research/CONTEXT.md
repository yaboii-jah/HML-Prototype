# Integrated Procurement Research

Vocabulary for comparing the selected literature with the present study's procurement workflow. These terms distinguish source findings, design similarities, and the scope of the research contribution.

## Language

**Integrated procurement in the present study:**
The connection of material requirements, assigned reviews, sourcing decisions, quotations, orders, and supporting records within the proposed procurement workflow.

**Centralized procurement information:**
Related procurement records made available through a shared source to authorized personnel across projects. This is distinct from centralized purchasing authority.
_Avoid_: Centralized purchasing when referring only to shared records

**Centralized purchasing authority:**
A procurement arrangement that concentrates purchasing decisions in a central organizational function.

**Specific similarity:**
A documented activity, information requirement, or assessment approach in a reviewed study that has an identifiable counterpart in the present study.
_Avoid_: Similar technology as the sole explanation of similarity

**Contextual difference:**
A difference in organizational setting, procurement responsibilities, or implementation conditions that affects how a reviewed study can inform the present study.
_Avoid_: Research gap when only a contextual difference has been identified

**Supported contextual gap:**
The need to implement and assess the proposed workflow in its intended setting, bounded by what the selected literature evaluates and by the present study's scope.
_Avoid_: Universal novelty, absence of all comparable systems

**Reported finding:**
A result or observation attributed to its source and qualified by that source's method, sample, and stated limitations.
_Avoid_: Proven benefit when the evidence is perception, association, or an uncontrolled result

**Design interpretation:**
An explicit application of a reviewed finding to the present study's proposed workflow. It is distinct from a feature or outcome directly evaluated by the reviewed authors.

**Functional and user assessment:**
Evaluation of the proposed system's intended functions and users' assessments of its suitability. It is distinct from before-and-after measurement of procurement time or costs.
